# Communication Planner Product Brief

## Purpose

Communication Planner is a 1-to-1 communication management app for professionals who need to maintain accurate, safe, person-specific conversations across multiple channels.

The app focuses on individual communication depth, not public reach. SNS Planner remains responsible for post creation and distribution.

## Primary User Problem

When a user talks to many customers at the same time, context can be mixed up. This creates risks such as:

- Replying with information meant for another person
- Forgetting what was promised
- Losing the next action
- Sending a reply before checking tone, content, and recipient fit
- Treating separate channel identities as separate people when they are actually the same person

## MVP Jobs

- Collect incoming channel messages into a unified inbox.
- Link channel identities to a communication person projection.
- Keep conversations and messages person-scoped.
- Maintain concise conversation context per person.
- Extract topics, promises, and next actions.
- Generate reply drafts only with explicit `personId` and `conversationId`.
- Require SafetyCheck before send.

## Non-Goals

Communication Planner does not own:

- Customer master data
- Lead or sales lifecycle
- Reservations
- Payments
- Revenue
- SNS post drafts
- Numeria reports
- Velvet long-term professional memory
- AI usage and billing

## Integration Position

Growth Engine owns business records such as Customer, Lead, Reservation, Payment, and Revenue.

Numeria Studio owns professional sessions and reports.

SNS Planner owns 1-to-many post and campaign-oriented creation.

AI Platform Core owns AI Activity, AI Usage, and AI Capability.

Platform Admin observes operational status only.
